2. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and operating the Service
  • AI transcription of meeting audio (using OpenAI Whisper)
  • We request read-only access to your Gmail to automatically detect and extract action items and tasks using AI. We strictly do not use this data for any other purposes.
  • Task and schedule management features
  • Sending push notifications
  • Service improvement and statistical analysis
  • Google user data is strictly used only to provide or improve user-facing features of the Denlog application (e.g., user authentication, profile display, and extracting tasks from Gmail).

3. Third-Party Services

Your data may be shared with the following services solely for providing the application features:

  • OpenAI: Audio transcription and task extraction via API (Zero data retention/No AI training)
  • Supabase: Data storage (EU servers)
  • RevenueCat: Subscription billing
  • Firebase: App analytics and notifications

We do not sell Google user data to third parties. We do not transfer or disclose your Google information to third parties for purposes other than the ones provided. Your data will never be used for targeted advertising, sold to data brokers, or used for training AI models.

4. Data Security

Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality of your data. We use encryption to protect your information both in transit and at rest. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and protected by Supabase's Row Level Security (RLS) policies.

5. Your Rights & Data Retention

We store your personal information for a period of time that is consistent with our business purposes. When the data retention period expires for a given type of data, we will delete or destroy it.

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access and modify your personal information
  • Request for your data to be deleted by deleting your account (How to delete your account)
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
